# Diagnose From Observation, Not the Prior

**Set the diagnosis from the cheapest direct observation, not from the prior
you walked in with.**

Fires when something is broken and you already have a theory: your own guess, a
reviewer's, a memory, a host's reputation. First read the signal already in hand,
since the status code, missing output or working sibling usually names the axis.
Name the observation that would falsify the theory and run it before you edit,
ship or document anything. Until then, write "hypothesis", never "cause". Read the
playbook,
[`~/.claude/rule-playbooks/diagnose-from-observation-not-prior.md`](~/.claude/rule-playbooks/diagnose-from-observation-not-prior.md),
for the mechanism, procedure and surfaces. Its sibling for builds is
[`premise-observed-not-inferred.md`](premise-observed-not-inferred.md).

## Red Flags

- "probably", "should be", "I know this host" or a handed-over theory, and no observation has run.
- You are about to document a root cause you inferred but did not observe.
- A discriminating signal is present and your reasoning never says what it rules out.
- Every step in your plan could confirm the prior and none could falsify it.
