# Break the False Binary — Reframe the Axis When Every Option Is Bad

**When every option is bad or worse, the axis is wrong: reframe a level up until
you reach the structural cause, not just a different-shaped guess.**

Fires when you find yourself ranking bad against worse. Your first reframe can
carry the bias that produced the binary; if it lands on "replace X with the
simpler Y", check it against
[`dont-read-churn-as-rot.md`](dont-read-churn-as-rot.md). The playbook,
[`~/.claude/rule-playbooks/break-the-false-binary.md`](~/.claude/rule-playbooks/break-the-false-binary.md),
holds the full pattern and DO/DON'T.

## Red Flags

- "A bad hack or an even worse one", or ranking bad-vs-worse internally.
- A third option you can't tie to a named structural cause.
- A large hand-maintained list partitioning an identifier space by a property:
  ask whether the identifier can carry the property itself.
